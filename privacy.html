MPG Social Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 11, 2026

MPG Social is an internal tool used only by the Multiplayer Games team to manage the team's own YouTube channel and X account. It is not offered to the public.

What it accesses

When a team member signs in with Google, MPG Social requests permission to upload videos to, manage, and read analytics for the team's own YouTube channel. When signing in with X, it requests permission to read the team's own profile and to publish posts and media to it.

How data is used and stored

Sign-in tokens are stored only on the team member's own computer and are used only to perform actions the team requests on its own accounts. MPG Social does not collect data about other users, does not sell or share any data with third parties, and does not use Google user data for advertising or to train AI models. Use of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

Removing access

Access can be revoked at any time from Google account permissions or X's connected apps settings, and deleting the locally stored token files removes all stored data.
